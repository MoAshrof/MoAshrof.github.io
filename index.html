BUY2026 · Strait of Hormuz

The strait

Group Head of Planning & Procurement, first weeks in the role

57 / 57
machines kept running through the closure

When the strait closed, 100% of our polymer came through the Gulf. Within 2–3 weeks we had other sources in the US, Russia and the local market, and stock cover went from 90 to 120 days.

  • ~$0.36M saved by renegotiating shipments bought at crisis prices.
  • ~600 MT bought at $190 per ton below market (~$0.11M).
